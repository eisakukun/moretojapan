Who Am I?

You choose a character, a thing or a creature for your partner — and they choose one for you. Your partner can see yours — you can't. Ask yes/no questions, answer theirs, and find out who you are before the time runs out.

1 · Topic & cardPick the topic by dice, by choice, or “everything”. Then choose who your partner will be — from the list, or type any character you like.
2 · Ask“Am I an animal?” “Can you eat me?” — tap a ready-made question in both languages, or say it out loud.
3 · GuessTry to answer in the language you are learning. 3 tries per round, 5 or 10 minutes, 1 to 3 rounds — most correct wins.

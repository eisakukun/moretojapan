What is sold

Credit or debit card (processed by Stripe). Card details are entered on Stripe's page and never reach this site.
Payment period
Your card is charged when you complete the payment, before the lesson.
Delivery time
Lessons take place at the date and time we agree on by message after payment. The 5-lesson pack has no expiry.
Cancellations and refunds
Before a lesson: tell me at least 24 hours ahead and you can move the lesson or get a full refund.
Less than 24 hours, or no-show: the lesson counts as used.
If I have to cancel: we rebook, or you get a full refund — your choice.
5-lesson pack: unused lessons can be refunded; lessons already taken are counted at the single-lesson price.
Tips: not refundable, since nothing is owed in return.
Refunds go back to the card you paid with.

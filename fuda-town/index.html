Fuda Town — the words are hiding

Fuda (札) means card. Somewhere in this town — inside a pot, under a vending machine, in the tall grass — there are word cards. Face something and press A to look. The rarer the card, the harder the Japanese: ★ is a simple word in kana, ★★★★★ is a proverb. Cards are also your moves in battle: pick one, answer what it means, and it hits. Rare cards hit harder — if you really know them. Hidden cards come back every day.

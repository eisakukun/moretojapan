A language exchange that starts with the game you already play.

A normal language exchange runs out of things to say in about twenty minutes, and then it is two people doing homework. A match does not run out. You are already shouting at the same screen, and half of what you need to say is already on it. Pick your game, say which language you have and which one you want, and talk while you play.

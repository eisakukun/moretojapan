Hinata Bar #

Four tables, sixteen seats. You talk by voice with your own table — the other tables can't hear you. When the timer ends, everyone is shuffled to a new table.

Which side are you?

Above your head everyone sees: your language ▸ the language you want to speak. You can hide the bubbles on your own screen, but you can't hide yours from other people.

Your browser will ask for the microphone. Without it you can still come in and listen.

Privacy

What this site knows about you

As little as possible. Here is exactly what that means, in plain words.

Last updated: 6 October 2026

The short version

  • No accounts.
  • No tracking cookies.
  • No ads today.
  • What you post is public.
  • Camera and voice stay on your side.

Visitor numbers

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It does not use cookies.

Saved only on your device

Some things are remembered in your browser so you do not have to set them again.

  • Your language choice.
  • A random device code.
  • Your profile, if you make one.
  • Copies of recent posts, likes, and a call pass.

What you post

Posts are stored in a Google spreadsheet run by this site. Everything except the device code is public.

Older posts drop out of the lists but are not automatically erased. Ask us and we will delete them.

A post reported by three people is hidden automatically.

Your email address (optional)

Your email is used only to tell you when someone replies. It is never shown.

Looking up meanings

The text you choose is sent to Google’s translation service.

Camera games

The camera picture is read inside your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Voice calls

Calls connect directly between two browsers and are not stored.

A call pass is only a code.

Other services this site loads

Pages load a few files from other companies.

  • GitHub Pages
  • Cloudflare
  • Google
  • jsDelivr / unpkg
  • PeerJS

Advertising

There are no ads today. This page will be updated before that changes.

Children

This site is not aimed at children under 13.

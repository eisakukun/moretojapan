Write to me

A question, a correction, something you want removed, or a place you think should be written about. It comes straight to me.

Before you write

  • To book a lesson, the trial lesson form asks the right questions.
  • To report a post on the boards, the report button on that post is fastest. A post reported by three people is hidden automatically.
  • To have your own post or information deleted, write here and say which one.

I usually reply within a day or two. There is no public email address on this page, on purpose: it keeps the inbox readable.

What happens to what you send is on the privacy page. Who runs this site is on the about page.

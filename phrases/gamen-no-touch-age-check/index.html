“画面のタッチをお願いします” — the age check when you buy alcohol in Japan

Buying beer at a Japanese convenience store, the clerk says “年齢確認のため、画面のタッチをお願いします”. Say “はい” and tap the screen to confirm you're 20+. Everyone taps, whatever their age.

The convenience store clerk says

年齢確認ねんれいかくにんのため、画面がめんのタッチをお願ねがいします。

Please tap the screen to confirm your age.

You say

はい。

Sure. (then tap the screen)

What it means

  • 年齢確認 = AGE CHECK
  • 画面 = SCREEN
  • Tap → I'm 20+

It's an age check. Just tap the button on the little screen at the register, to say you're over twenty.

Good to know

It's not about how you look. Everyone taps, even if you're clearly sixty.

Say it slowly

画面がめんのタッチ

ga · me · n · no · ta · cchi
